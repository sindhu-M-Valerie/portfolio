CASE No. 05

Jarvis

Problem A chatbot that answers legal questions is only useful if its answers are correct, complete and consistent.

Outcome A repeatable way to test an AI agent's answers against a golden set of reference answers.

Full case notes

What it is. An evaluation workflow for testing the answers of an AI agent built to answer legal questions.

How it works. I create a golden data set of reference answers to representative legal questions. The agent's answers are then checked against it by human review for accuracy, hallucinations and policy compliance, and errors are categorized so recurring weaknesses can be fixed.

Why it matters. It turns "the chatbot seems fine" into measured, auditable evidence.
